Privacy Policy

Ensuring your data is safe with LiveMenu

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to our users:

  • Account Information: When you sign in via Google OAuth, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Kitchen Data: Information about your kitchen, including menu items, prices, and contact details provided by you.
  • Customer Orders: Phone numbers and order details provided by end-users to facilitate delivery.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information collected from Google to authenticate your identity and provide access to your specific kitchen dashboard. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Retention

We retain your personal information as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting our support team.

This policy is designed to comply with Google OAuth API Services User Data Policy.
